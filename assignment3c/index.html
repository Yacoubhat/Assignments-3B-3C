Interactive Data Visualisation

Healthcare Spending & Life Expectancy

Exploring global health through interactive data visualisation

Healthcare systems vary considerably across countries in how much they spend and the health outcomes they achieve. This interactive data visualisation explores the relationship between healthcare expenditure and life expectancy, how these measures have changed over time, and how patterns differ between countries.

Use the interactive stories below to select countries, move through years, compare trends and inspect individual observations behind the broader global patterns.

About the data

From international health data to interactive stories

The visualisations bring together datasets covering healthcare expenditure, life expectancy and public health spending. The data has been cleaned and prepared so observations can be explored and compared by country and year.

Measures used across the project include healthcare expenditure per person adjusted for purchasing power parity (PPP), total life expectancy at birth, male and female life expectancy, and public health expenditure measures.

Source datasets do not always contain exactly the same countries or years. Each interactive story therefore uses the observations available for the measures being compared. Relationships shown in the visualisations should be interpreted as associations rather than evidence of causation.
